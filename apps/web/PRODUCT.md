# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Developers and job-seekers who keep a résumé as structured JSON, tailor it against job descriptions with an LLM, and export a repeatable PDF. They work in a dashboard of sections (experience, skills, projects) — often late at night, iterating quickly.

## Product Purpose

Keep résumé data as owned JSON: edit by hand or with any assistant, remix sections across versions, and export the same layout every time. Success is a résumé you can change without fighting a document.

## Brand Personality

Structured, dry, a little geometric. Forest green and folded-document mark. Tool-first, not marketing-first.

## Anti-references

Generic DaisyUI alert cards, SaaS cream dashboards, loud error pages with red-on-black panic, gradient-text “oops” screens.

## Design Principles

- The JSON is the source of truth; the UI is a workbench around it.
- Familiar product patterns over invented chrome.
- Brand green for selection and primary action, not decoration.
- Errors should feel like the rest of the app: calm, recoverable, with real details in development.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA contrast on body text. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. Keyboard-reachable actions on error and empty states.
